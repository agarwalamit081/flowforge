# FlowForge

**Tagline:** Transform overwhelm into actions and rewire procrastination into momentum.

A cross‑platform (Windows/macOS/Linux) desktop application that lives in your system tray, quietly observing your work patterns, calendar, and task list.
It uses real‑time context and psychological insight to dismantle the root causes of procrastination the moment they appear. It combines passive awareness (periodic screenshots, app/website tracking, calendar sync) with a suite of over 25 interactive modules that together act as a task coach, emotional regulator, and accountability partner.

---

## Core Philosophy
> *"Don't fight procrastination—understand it, then gently redirect it."*

It uses behavioral science, AI task decomposition, and compassionate nudges to help users build sustainable momentum by addressing root causes and transforms overwhelm into actions—not just symptoms.

## How It Works: The Core Loop

1.  **Morning Briefing (Daily Intent Setter)**
    The day starts with a non‑intrusive, 5‑minute check‑in. FlowForge pulls today's events from Google calendar and agenda tracker (part of this app). It asks: *"What 1–3 outcomes would make today feel like a win?"* Your answers are turned into SMART goals (**Goal Clarifier**) and become the day's North Star.

2.  **Always‑On Observation**
    FlowForge takes periodic, screenshots (privacy‑safe) of your active window (processed entirely on‑device; faces and sensitive content to be automatically blurred). It also silently logs app switches and website visits (**Distraction Log**). Combined with calendar data, it builds a real‑time picture: are you in a scheduled focus block, a meeting, or aimlessly scrolling?

3.  **Task & Project Intake**
    You capture tasks naturally – by typing. The built‑in agenda tracker unifies everything. When a task feels vague or heavy, FlowForge's **Task Deconstructor + Micro‑Task Breaker** AI slices it into micro‑tasks with a glowing "Start Here" button. For complex projects, a **Visual Roadmapper** generates an interactive flowchart, making the unknown navigable.

4.  **Live Intervention Engine**
    As you work, FlowForge's **Procrastination Pattern Detector** learns your unique avoidance footprints (e.g., you open social media after 45 minutes of deep work, or you freeze when a task's first step is unclear). When a risk pattern is detected – or you manually hit the "I'm Stuck" (**Unstick Me**) button – the app activates the right intervention from its toolbox, at the right moment.

## Privacy-First Design

1.  **All screenshots processed locally**: No images leave the device unless explicitly shared by user
2.  **Opt-in granularity**: Users choose which apps/windows are monitored; sensitive apps (banking, health) auto-excluded
3.  **Blur-by-default**: Faces, personal text, and URLs auto-redacted in any processed visual data
4.  **Transparent dashboard**: Real-time view of what data is collected, why, and when it's deleted
5.  **One-click data purge**: Full local data deletion anytime

## The Toolbox: How the 25+ Features Unite

Rather than a jumble of separate tools, FlowForge's features are woven into a single, adaptive experience:

### Tackling "Task is too large / vague / daunting"
*   **Task Deconstructor + Micro-Task Breaker** – AI delivers a sequence of bite‑size steps (5-15 min micro-tasks) and highlights the absolute easiest physical action (e.g., "Open a blank document and title it").
*   **Visual Roadmapper** – Overwhelming projects become collapsible flowcharts with 5–10 minute nodes; progress pulses as items complete to visually reinforce momentum.
*   **Starting Point Generator ("Unstick Me")** – When stuck, click "Unstick Me" → suggests lowest-friction entry: *"Open the document. Don't write—just title it."*
*   **"Good Enough" Coach + Outcome Visualizer** – Before you start, it asks: *"What's the minimum viable version that would be useful?"* You define minimal completion criteria. You can sketch or describe the desired result; FlowForge reverse‑engineers the steps, lowering the barrier to just "good enough."

### Solving "Lack of clear goals / decision fatigue"
*   **Goal Clarifier + Daily Intent Setter** – Vague intentions become crystallized with measurable outcomes each morning using `SMART`. It specifically asks: *"What does 'done' look like? What's the smallest measurable outcome?"* to convert "work on report" into specific actions.
*   **Priority Auto‑Sorter** – Each morning, FlowForge applies an Eisenhower Matrix + your historical energy patterns (when you're most focused) to rank today's tasks, eliminating choice overload.
*   **Template Library** – Recurring work (weekly reports, email triage) gets pre‑built micro‑workflows; you just press play.

### Eradicating "Poor time estimation / unrealistic planning"
*   **Reality Check Timer + Buffer Builder** – Compares your past estimates with real completion times on similar tasks, then auto‑suggests padded deadlines and contingency blocks based on your historical patterns.
*   **Time‑Block Assistant** – Scans your google calendar, finds optimal slots matching a task's required focus level and duration, and books them with one click.
*   **Time Travel Planner** – A side‑by‑side simulation of your stress/schedule if you act now vs. later – a visceral nudge: *"If you start now: finish by 4pm, low stress. If you wait: rush tonight, high stress."*

### Restoring "Interest & self‑efficacy"
*   **Pair-Task Matcher** – For a boring report, it suggests pairing with a favorite playlist, podcast, or lo-fi beats, and can launch both.
*   **Confidence Builder + Skill Scaffold** – When you doubt your ability, it shows past wins (e.g., *"You completed X similar task last month"*) and offers just-in-time micro-tutorials (e.g., "Here's a 2‑minute guide on pivot tables").
*   **Peer Proof Viewer** – Anonymized, community‑sourced "first messy drafts" of similar projects remind you that everyone starts imperfectly.
*   **Task Gamifier + Achievement Unlocks** – Earn XP for completed micro‑tasks and streak badges.

### Closing the "Accountability gap"
*   **Virtual Accountability Partner** – Features flexible options including an AI persona or optional peer matching for check-ins. The app shares progress screenshots (user-approved) and delivers motivational messages tied to *your* long-term goals.
*   **Progress Screenshot Timelapse** – End‑of‑day recap that compresses your work window into a 30‑second video (privacy-safe, blurred content) – undeniable proof of movement.
*   **Five-Minute Rule Enforcer** – Highlights sub‑5‑minute tasks and nudges you: *"This takes less time than scrolling—do it now?"* to prevent small-task accumulation.

### Dismantling "Emotional avoidance & perfectionism"
*   **Emotion Check‑In + Compassionate Prompter** – A quick 10-second mood logger triggers a tailored strategy: 60-second breathing (Mindful Start Ritual), a reframing phrase, or a micro‑break. It replaces self-criticism with evidence-based encouragement.
*   **Procrastination Journal** – With one click, you answer *"What's stopping you right now?"* The app suggests an evidence-based coping strategy (e.g., if it's anxiety → 4‑7‑8 breathing; if it's boredom → pair‑task).
*   **Anti‑Procrastination Chatbot** – A Socratic AI that asks gentle questions ("What would need to be true for you to take action right now?") and helps you break the avoidance loop.

### Reflection & Growth
*   **Momentum Tracker** – A visual "chain" of productive days that leverages "don't break the chain" psychology to maintain streaks.
*   **Mindful Start Ritual** – A 60-second guided breathing/visualization to transition into work mode and reduce activation energy.

## The Always‑On Intelligence: Screenshots, Calendars & Agenda

*   **Privacy‑First Screenshots:** Captured only from the active work window, processed entirely on‑device. Sensitive data is automatically blurred. The user can review and delete any screenshot. The AI analyzes visual patterns – e.g., an empty code editor for 20 minutes – not raw text content. This data fuels the Procrastination Pattern Detector and the Progress Timelapse without compromising privacy.
*   **Calendar Sync (Google):** FlowForge knows your hard landscape – meetings, appointments, deadlines. It never double‑books, respects busy status, and uses free time for suggested focus blocks.
*   **Agenda Tracker:** A unified, lightweight task list that syncs with the app's deconstruction engine. You can drag emails in, dictate a task. Statuses (not started, in progress, stuck, done) are visualized and influence intervention timing.

## Refined Anti‑Procrastination Architecture

All modules communicate through a central **Context Manager** that uses the data stream to answer: *What is the user meant to be doing, what are they actually doing, and what emotional state are they likely in?* This manager then deploys features in concert:

*   **Focus Timer + Distraction Blocker:** When a focus block starts, FlowForge can optionally block distracting sites/apps. Gentle Pomodoro-style rhythm reminders ("You're 12 minutes in – keep the rhythm") prevent drift.
*   **Distraction Log:** Neutral logging of context switches; a 5‑second reflection dot ("Was this necessary?") appears later to prompt self-correction.
*   **Emotion Check‑In + Mindful Start Ritual:** At the beginning of each task block, you're invited to a 60‑second centering routine, drastically lowering the entry barrier.

The result is an application that doesn't just *list* solutions – it delivers the right one at the precise moment of need, turning the daily battle against procrastination into a guided, compassionate, and ultimately automatic process of re‑engagement.

All modules communicate through a central **Context Manager** implemented as a Rust struct with internal channels.

```
  Activity Monitor ──►
```

- **Core loop:** The Context Manager continuously evaluates the user’s “meant‑to” state (from calendar + top task) vs. “actual” state (active app, URL, idle time).  
- When a mismatch or a stuck pattern is detected, it queries the **Intervention Rules Engine** to choose the least intrusive, most effective module (e.g., “Five‑Minute Rule” if the top task is quick, or “Unstick Me” prompt if the user has been idle in a text editor).  
- Events are sent to the frontend via Tauri’s event system, causing a tray notification or an in‑app overlay.

## Technical Stack

1. **Core Framework**: Tauri v2 for Desktop Container

Tauri uses the system’s native webview, resulting in significantly smaller binaries and lower RAM usage.
Tauri’s backend in Rust provides memory safety and high-speed processing for the on-device screenshot blurring and pattern detection required by FlowForge.

**Key Tauri features to use:**
- **System tray** with menu and window management.  
- **Commands + events** for frontend‑backend communication.  
- **Plugins:** `tauri-plugin-sql` (or raw `rusqlite`), `tauri-plugin-fs`, `tauri-plugin-shell`, `tauri-plugin-oauth` (for Google sign‑in).  
- **Custom protocol** to serve locally stored, blurred screenshots securely to the UI.

Tauri’s bundler creates:
- Windows: `.msi` / `.nsis` installer  
- macOS: `.dmg` with mandatory code‑signing  
- Linux: `.deb`, `.AppImage`, or `.rpm`  

2. **The Frontend (The "Toolbox") UI & State Management**

The suite of over 25 interactive modules needs a flexible, reactive UI.
**Framework**: React 18+ with TypeScript

**State Management**: Zustand

**Styling**: Tailwind CSS. Perfect for creating the "compassionate" and "non-intrusive" aesthetic FlowForge aims for.

| Layer           | Recommendation                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Framework       | React 18+ with TypeScript                             |
| Build tool      | Vite                                                  |
| Styling         | Tailwind CSS (rapid, consistent design)               |
| State management| Zustand (lightweight, minimal boilerplate)            |
| Routing         | React Router v6 (for settings, timelapse viewer)      |
| Charts/visuals  | Recharts (momentum tracker), React Flow (Visual Roadmapper) |

The frontend is a **single‑page application** loaded by Tauri. It communicates exclusively through Tauri’s `invoke` and listens to events emitted by the Rust backend (e.g., new distraction logged, intervention triggered).

3. The "Always-On" Backend (Rust)
The Rust core will handle the heavy lifting to ensure privacy and speed.  

**System Hooks**: Use the rdev or revents crates to listen for keyboard/mouse activity and window_titles (or native platform APIs) for the Distraction Log and app switching.  

**Screenshot Engine**: A custom Rust module using the cap crate to capture active windows.  

**Privacy Layer**: Use ONNX Runtime (via ort) to run small, local machine learning models (like YOLO or Moondream) to identify and blur faces or sensitive text before saving to the local database.


Asynchronous Runtime & Foundation
- **Tokio** – async runtime for all background services (screenshot scheduler, calendar sync, LLM).  
- **Serde** – serialize/deserialize all IPC payloads.  
- **Chrono** – date/time handling for calendar and scheduling.  

Database & Persistent Storage
- **SQLite** via **rusqlite** – stores agenda, tasks, user settings, distraction log, emotion check‑ins, and AI model metadata.  
- Screenshots stored directly on disk, with paths in the database; encrypted at rest using **`aes-gcm`** crate if the user desires optional encryption.

Active Window & Activity Monitoring
- **`active-win-pos-rs`** (or `active-win` crate) – retrieves active window title, app name, and geometry cross‑platform.  
- **`xcap`** (Windows), **`core-graphics`** (macOS), **`x11`** / **`wayland`** (Linux) for screen capture.  
  - Unify through a thin abstraction: capture full virtual screen, crop to window bounding box using the `image` crate.  
- **Global hotkeys** – `tauri-plugin-global-shortcut` for the “Unstick Me” hotkey.

Browser Extension Integration
- The Tauri app runs a **local WebSocket server** (`tokio-tungstenite`) on `localhost`.  
- A companion browser extension (Chrome/Firefox) sends the current URL and page title to this server.  
- The extension also enforces website blocking (it can redirect or close tabs on command). This avoids the complexity of system‑level traffic interception.

Calendar Sync
- **Google Calendar API** via OAuth 2.0. Use `tauri-plugin-oauth` to handle the sign‑in flow, then use **`reqwest`** with the obtained token to fetch events.  
- Sync runs periodically in a background tokio task; diffs are stored locally.

Screenshot Processing & Privacy Engine
This is the most security‑critical module. All processing happens **on‑device** using the following stack:

| Component            | Library / Model                                        |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Image I/O & cropping | **`image`** crate (PNG/JPEG)                          |
| Face detection       | **`ort`** (ONNX Runtime) with an UltraFace or YOLOv8n-face model (≤5 MB) |
| Face blurring        | **`imageproc`** crate – draw filled blur circles over detected regions |
| Text/OCR detection   | **`leptess`** (Tesseract bindings) – recognizes text; then apply regex for emails, credit cards, URLs, personal names |
| Text redaction       | Overwrite matching regions with black boxes or Gaussian blur |

On‑Device LLM for Task Deconstruction & Coaching
The app’s AI features (Task Deconstructor, Anti‑Procrastination Chatbot, Goal Clarifier, etc.) require a capable, private language model.

**Recommended engine:**  
- **`llama-cpp-rs`** (bindings to llama.cpp) – loads quantised GGUF models, runs efficiently on CPU.  
- Model: **Llama‑3.2‑3B‑Instruct Q4_K_M** (~2 GB on disk) or **Phi‑3.5‑mini‑instruct Q4** – small enough for background use while still following complex prompts.  

**Integration pattern:**
- A single `LlmSession` struct is created on app start, loading the model into memory once.  
- All LLM calls are dispatched via a tokio channel, processed sequentially to keep memory under control.  
- Prompts are carefully engineered for each module; context from the Context Manager is injected (current task, user’s past similar tasks, etc.).  
- For simple, repetitive tasks (like “make this goal SMART”), you can cache prompts and even fine‑tune a tiny rule‑based fallback to reduce LLM load.

Procrastination Pattern Detector
- Implement as a **rule engine + lightweight ML** system.  
- **Rules:** e.g., “After 45 min of VS Code, if browser URL matches social media, trigger a gentle nudge”.  
- **Learning:** Use a simple decision tree (e.g., `smartcore` crate) trained on the user’s past distraction events (features: time of day, active application before switch, task urgency). This personalises interventions without heavy deep learning.  
- The classifier can output a risk score; when it exceeds a threshold, the Context Manager selects an intervention.

Time‑Block Assistant & Scheduler
- Use a Rust constraint‑satisfaction library (or hand‑roll a simple bin‑packing algorithm) to fit tasks into available calendar gaps.  
- **`chrono`** and **`icalendar`** crate for parsing Google Calendar data.

Progress Timelapse Generation
- Collect the blurred screenshots taken during focus sessions.  
- Use **`ffmpeg`** sidecar (bundled with the app, or via `ffmpeg-next` crate) to stitch them into a 30‑second MP4.  
- Provide the video file path to the frontend for playback in the end‑of‑day review.

### Environmental & Integration Details

#### Local-First Data Strategy
To fulfill the **Privacy-First Design**:
*   **Database:** Use **SQLite** for structured data (tasks, logs). It’s robust, local, and allows for the "One-click data purge" feature.
*   **Encryption:** Use `sqlcipher` to ensure that even if the device is compromised, the user's behavioral data remains encrypted at rest.

#### The Intervention Engine
The **Context Manager** should be implemented as a Rust "sidecar" or a background thread within Tauri. It will constantly evaluate the stream of activity against the user's scheduled focus blocks. When it detects a "Procrastination Pattern," it sends a message via **Tauri Events** to the frontend to trigger a "Compassionate Prompt" or "Unstick Me" module.

#### External APIs
*   **Calendar Sync:** Use the `google-calendar3` crate (Rust) or a standard OAuth2 flow within the frontend to pull events for the **Morning Briefing**.
*   **AI Decomposition:** While local LLMs (like Llama 3 via `llama.cpp`) are getting better, you might consider an optional, encrypted bridge to an API (like OpenAI or Anthropic) for the **Task Deconstructor** if high-level reasoning is required, though the prompt emphasizes on-device processing.

This architecture ensures FlowForge is a "quiet observer" that is lightning-fast, respects the user's privacy, and provides a seamless bridge between their calendar and their actual work habits.
